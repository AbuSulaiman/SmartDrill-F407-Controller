INTRODUCTION
As devices shrink toward atomic scale, the silicon based electronics are hitting fundamental physical 
limits: heat dissipation, quantum interference, and leakage current. New materials are needed.
Why Spintronics? Using the electron's intrinsic spin (↑ or ↓) alongside its charge as an information 
carrier could enable faster, more energy efficient devices.
Graphene: It exhibits exceptionally high carrier mobility, quantum mechanical effects at room 
temperature, long spin lifetime.
MOIRÉ PATTERN
AMoiré pattern:
Is the periodic interference pattern created
when two layered material are stacked with
a twist angle θ between their lattices, and
this pattern itself forms a chiral, periodic
potential that can filter electron spin, with
+θ and −θ giving opposite handedness, and
0° giving no chirality at all.
In our device, this occurs between two
graphene layers: for angles between 0°
and 30°.
CISS EFFECT
Chirality:
describes a structure that cannot be
superimposed on its mirror image, like a left and

The Measurements
We will measure the voltage across the Ni contact with its
magnetization pointing up, then down, using an external field to flip
it. Because Ni senses one spin direction more easily than the other,
this voltage should differ between the two magnetization directions
if the interface is genuinely filtering spin; a zero difference would
mean no filtering at all. We will repeat this at five twist angles 0°,
+15°, −15°, +30°, −30° to see how, or whether, the effect
depends on the twist.
